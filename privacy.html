Glassboard Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026. Glassboard 1.0 for iPhone and iPad.

The short version

Glassboard does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you copy stays on your device and in your own iCloud, where only you can read it.

What Glassboard keeps

Glassboard keeps a history of the things you copy or share to it: text, links, photos, PDFs and the details it finds in them, such as addresses, phone numbers and one-time codes. This history is stored on your device. If iCloud is on, it is also stored in your own iCloud account (the app’s private iCloud container) so your other devices can show it. The developer has no access to your iCloud and cannot read your clippings.

What Glassboard does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • It has no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code.
  • It does not ask you to sign in or create an account.
  • It reads the clipboard only when you open it, and only after iOS lets it (iOS shows “Allow Paste”, or you can set Paste from Other Apps in Settings). It never reads it in the background.

Things that happen on your device

  • Search and suggestions run on your device, using the language models built into it. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Links. To show a copied link’s title and picture, your device loads that page itself, the way a browser would. The page’s own site can see that request. Glassboard sends nothing else, and links you copy are not shared with the developer or anyone else.
  • Secrets. Text that looks like a password, a key or a one-time code is kept out of suggestions and previews.
  • Photos. Glassboard can add a photo to your library when you ask it to (“Save”). It cannot read the rest of your library.
  • Weather Sky is optional and off until you turn it on. If you do, your device asks Apple’s weather service for the weather at an approximate location and draws the sky. No location is kept or shared by Glassboard.
  • Widgets and Live Activities show your latest clipping from data kept on your device. On a locked screen they show only what a clipping is, never its words.
  • Performance reports. iOS can give Glassboard anonymous performance reports. They stay on your device. You may share one from Settings, and then only through the share sheet you choose.

Your control

You choose how long clippings are kept, you can delete any clipping or clear the whole history, and deleting is final: a deleted clipping does not come back from another device. Turning iCloud off for Glassboard in iOS Settings stops syncing. Deleting the app removes its data from your device; to remove the copy in iCloud, delete it in Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage > Glassboard.

Children

Glassboard is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes
