Glassboard Support

Help for Glassboard 1.0 on iPhone and iPad.

Need a hand?

Write to kb@korrybusch.com. Please say which iPhone or iPad you use, your iOS version and what you were doing. Do not send anything you copied that you would not want read.

Getting started

  • Copy something in any app, then open Glassboard. iOS asks to allow the paste; choose Allow. To stop the question, open Settings > Apps > Glassboard > Paste from Other Apps and choose Allow.
  • Tap a clipping to copy it again. Touch and hold a row for its actions.
  • Pin clippings you reach for often: swipe a row to the right. Swipe to the left to delete it; Undo appears for a moment.

Saving in a tap

  • Share sheet: choose Save to Glassboard from any app’s share sheet.
  • Control Center, Lock Screen, Action Button and Back Tap: in Glassboard, open Settings and look under Save from anywhere; each row shows how to add it. (The Action Button and Back Tap are on iPhone only.) iOS lets only the app that is open read the clipboard, so each of these opens Glassboard for a moment. Tap the back button at the top of the screen to return.
  • Widgets: add the Clippings widget to the Home Screen or the Clipping widget to the Lock Screen to keep your latest clipping one tap away.

Two pages when there is room

What do I see when I open a foldable flat?

Two pages, one on each side of the fold. Your history is on the first page. The second page shows the clipping you last copied; until you copy or choose one it shows a quiet card. Nothing is drawn across the fold itself.

What goes on the second page?

Whatever you are working on: the clipping you just copied, the Copy As or Clipping Info page you opened from a row’s menu, the pages of Settings, or the pile of clippings you are choosing for Copy as One. They replace each other there, so nothing opens over both pages.

Tapping a clipping copies it. How do I just look at one?

Choose Show on the Second Page from the row’s menu, or use its VoiceOver action. It shows the clipping there without copying it.

How does Copy As work with two pages?

Open the row’s menu and choose Copy As. The options appear on the second page beside the clipping you started from, instead of a sheet over everything. Pick one and it copies; Cancel puts the second page back.

What happens if I fold it shut?

The outer screen shows one page, the way a phone does. Your search, your open clipping and your place in the list stay where they were; unfold and the second page is back.

Why are the buttons at the side on the outer screen?

That is the system’s own layout for the outer screen: it puts the tab bar and toolbars in a bar at the side, and Glassboard keeps its list clear of it. Other apps do the same.

Can I drag a clipping into the app beside it?

Yes. Touch and hold a clipping on either page and drag it into another app. Nothing is copied until you drop it.

Is anything shown when someone looks over my shoulder or I switch apps?

The app switcher shows a plain card, never a clipping, and the second page goes quiet when the app is not active. Turn on Hide While Sharing Screen in Settings to cover Glassboard while the screen is shared or recorded.

Common questions

Why does iOS ask to paste every time?

iOS asks until you allow it. Open Settings > Apps > Glassboard > Paste from Other Apps and choose Allow, and it will not ask again. Glassboard reads the clipboard only when you open it.

Why did a copy not appear?

If Paste was not allowed, Glassboard cannot see what you copied. Allow it as above. Items that are too large, or a kind Glassboard cannot read, are not saved, and the app says so.

Do my clippings sync?

Yes, between your devices that use the same iCloud account, if iCloud is on for Glassboard. Sign in to iCloud and check Settings > your name > iCloud > Apps Using iCloud. If iCloud is unreachable Glassboard keeps working and says so.

Can I search by describing what I remember?

Yes, on devices that support on-device language models. Other devices still search the words in your clippings.

How do I delete a clipping, or everything?

Swipe a row to the left to delete it, or open Settings in Glassboard and choose Clear Unpinned History or Clear All History. You choose how long clippings are kept in the same place.

Is any of my data collected?

No. Read the privacy policy.
